Good Product and Reliable your Supplier Sedulur Berkah Alam

Founded in 2025 by Zarkasyi, the organization was established to tap into the vast potential of abundant natural resources that have yet to be fully utilized. Our mission is to facilitate the collection, processing, and distribution of these resources, creating a more efficient value chain. By doing so, we empower local farmers and small-scale traders to access broader markets, while also providing businesses worldwide with a reliable supply of high-quality raw materials to support their production needs.

By collaborating with farmers, local communities, and the government, we aim to build a sustainable businesses that benefits all stakeholders, including buyers, while prioritizing environmental responsibility and the preservation of nature.
