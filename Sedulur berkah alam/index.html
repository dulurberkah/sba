Sedulur Berkah Alam Leaves and Spices Supplier

Our products are made from 100% natural ingredients, completely free from additives and chemical substances. Sourced directly from the gardens of local Indonesian farmers, our materials are handled with care to preserve their purity, freshness, and natural quality. We are dedicated to sustainable and ethical sourcing, ensuring that every product reflects the richness of nature.

Our company is officially licensed by the Republic of Indonesia and operates in compliance with national and international standards. We hold key certifications including the Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Analysis (CoA), Halal Certificate, Health Certificate, Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS), and FDA Registration, Invoice and Packing list, Bill of Landing, Custom Export Declaration and Export Authorization Note (NOE). These certifications demonstrate our commitment to delivering safe, high-quality, and trustworthy natural products to both domestic and global markets.
